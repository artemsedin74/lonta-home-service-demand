# Citation and provenance

Lonta AI (2026). *Home-Service Demand Study: home age, professional work and spending*.
Version 1.0. Canonical report: https://lonta.io/research/home-service-demand/

Original secondary analysis by Lonta AI, prepared with OpenAI Codex. The source
copy used is DataLumos 217661/V2: https://doi.org/10.3886/E217661V2. Credit Census/HUD
for the 2023 American Housing Survey, ICPSR/DataLumos for that archival edition,
and Lonta for the original cross-tabulations, comparisons and figures. The archive
DOI identifies the input copy, not a DOI or endorsement of this Lonta report.

When reusing a number, keep its service category, denominator, preceding 24-month
window, 2023 interview vintage, uncertainty and professional-work definition.
A project spending quartile is not a confidence interval. A count/spending share
comparison is not a causal effect or a 2026 price/revenue forecast.
